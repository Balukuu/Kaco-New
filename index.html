What we do

Services & Solutions

Precision geospatial technology and lifecycle support for surveying, mapping and construction across Africa.

GNSS & Total Stations

Trimble and Spectra Geospatial RTK receivers and robotic total stations for millimetre-accurate positioning.

Enterprise Drones

DJI Matrice platforms for LiDAR mapping, thermal and multispectral surveys.

3D Laser Scanning

Handheld SLAM scanners that capture real-time coloured point clouds of any site.

Ground Penetrating Radar

US Radar GPR to locate subsurface pipes, cables and utilities without digging.

Hydrographic Survey

Autonomous survey vessels for bathymetric and hydrographic data collection.

GIS & Spatial Infrastructure

Esri ArcGIS deployment, data harmonisation and enterprise GIS, plus Carlson and Autodesk software.

Calibration & Repairs

Certified optical and EDM calibration, priority repair and preventative maintenance plans.

Training & Support

Hands-on RTK, robotic station and drone pilot training, with phone and field technical support.

Contract Surveying

Geodetic networks, photogrammetry and corridor mapping delivered by experienced teams.
